# Two-turn chat format vs the single-prompt replication and the paper

Positions are named by their place in the prompt. The first token after the answer is the post-answer newline (PANL) in the paper's single prompt and the `<end_of_turn>` token of the model's answer turn in the two-turn format; the last prompt token is the confidence colon (CC) and the newline that opens the model's reply. Layer numbers after @ are where the peak sits. Generated by `scripts/tt_analyze.py`; raw numbers in `results/tt/summary.json`.
